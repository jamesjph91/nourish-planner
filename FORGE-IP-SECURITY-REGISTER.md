## 1. Copyright ownership and evidence

Original eligible artwork, photographs, written text and software generally attract copyright protection **automatically** in the UK. There is **no UK copyright registry** to purchase a certificate from. © notices and watermarking do not create protection. Whether a particular asset qualifies, and who owns it, can depend on how it was made, its originality and any employment/commission/AI-service terms. **Do not label a rights holder without confirming ownership**.

For each protected or licensed asset retain:
- Original editable master (SVG, source file, layered art, footage, design source), not merely a web-optimised copy.
- Original creator(s), dates and supporting drafts/commits and the terms for any AI tool used.
- Original licence and proof of permitted **commercial** use for fonts, photos, mockups, illustrations, templates and third-party code.
- Written assignment / licence from contractors or collaborators before relying on any newly commissioned art.
- SHA-256 checksum or immutable content hash and dated backed-up copies of actual master files.
- UK intended owner/operating legal entity, verified by the founder. Do not invent the owner.

## 2. Watermark policy

- Publicly posted promotional renders and garment **concept previews**: add a discreet but visible transparent identity watermark, ideally repeated on shareable high-resolution preview samples when appropriate, **only after the chosen name and logo are legally cleared**.
- Social photographs / video: include consistent branded bug in corners and copyright/credit information in exported metadata where supported. Do not claim ownership over third-party photographs or music.
- Public web-store product photographs: watermark **selectively**, balancing protection with buying clarity and mobile readability. Keep licensing and master evidence.
- **Never watermark original SVG masters, print production transfers, embroidery files or app icons**. Keep clean production-ready masters in controlled private storage. Watermarks do not prevent recreations, cropping or trade mark conflicts.
- Preserve rights metadata (creator, copyright owner **after verified**, year, licence, contact where appropriate), dated versions and checksums for deliverables.
- Avoid embedding personal user details, emails or addresses into public watermarks without the owner's decision.

**Provisional copyright notice format ONLY after holder confirmation:** © 2026 [VERIFIED LEGAL RIGHTS HOLDER]. All rights reserved. No notice or registration substitutes for clearance.
