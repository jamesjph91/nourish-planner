# FORGE — Brand, Merchandise & Commercial Launch Gates

**Prepared:** 26 October 2026. **Purpose:** a practical parallel delivery track while app engineering continues. **State:** design research and *unbranded* sample research permitted; final trading identity, logo ownership and bulk garment production **not cleared**.

## 1. Naming & IP strategy

The codebase currently uses **FORGE** as a provisional technical identifier. Existing potential third-party fitness-software and apparel rights documented in [FORGE-IP-SECURITY-REGISTER.md](FORGE-IP-SECURITY-REGISTER.md) create a material brand-conflict risk. A shortlist of 23 alternative possibilities exists, all un-cleared:

FENVA, UPTO, UPVO, FUVEN, FENHART, ASHTERN, STEDOR, FELLOR, ROOKELL, BRAYNOR, ARDVEL, BRAYVOR, TARWICK, HORTHEN, STORVE, STORN, BRONN, STORNE, THRAV, WROTH, STYRTH, FENWROTH, STRYTH.

No name is owned or available by virtue of appearing on this list.

**Progressive decision gate:**
1. Rank candidates by premium British/global fit, memorability, clear spoken spelling, versatility and search distinctiveness. Similarity risk is a veto, not a score one can offset with creative appeal.
2. Run preliminary exact, phonetic, apparel, fitness app, company, marketplace, handle and domain checks, recording search date and scope.
3. Engage a qualified UK trademark attorney for definitive relevant-class, common-law, territorial and lookalike advice. Avoid expensive production until written clearance.
4. Confirm which legal entity/person owns the final mark, licensed art and code. Obtain written assignments from any contributors.
5. Only then create and protect final clean master identity assets, reserve relevant domains and set launch naming.

Follow [issue #8](https://github.com/jamesjph91/nourish-planner/issues/8). This document does not replace legal advice.

## 2. Apparel development — safe parallel work

- Define a minimal collection: 1 training tee, 1 heavyweight hoodie, 1 training short. Treat this as a **research option**, not a purchase authorisation.
- Get sample quotations for two or three UK/EU/overseas factories, comparable fabrics/GSM, cut, finishes, size grading, stitching, embroidery/printing, wash durability, ethical sourcing and supply lead times.
- Order **unbranded** test samples only after approval of a capped sample budget; photograph fit/finish and retain measurements.
- Model landed cost = garment + decoration + inbound freight + duties + QC + packaging; model contribution margin after payment, fulfillment, returns, tax and marketing, using verified quotes.
- Build size guide and returns process before buying stock. UK distance-selling cancellation obligations and applicable textile labels must be reviewed.
- Do not post mock branded campaigns or claim trademarks while clearance is outstanding.

Follow [issue #15](https://github.com/jamesjph91/nourish-planner/issues/15).

## 3. Website and monetisation launch track

- Reserve a legally viable domain **after** naming gate, then design a minimal waitlist/marketing site with compliant opt-in practices.
- Avoid collecting health data into marketing lists. Keep newsletters and application accounts separate unless users have clear choices.
- Create commerce product pages, inventory and fulfillment procedures; choose providers only after comparing fees and contractual fit.
- Prepare subscription tiers and entitlements in design; production payment acceptance is blocked pending the app's authentication, security and legal gates.
- Set up verified customer support, written service terms and an incident escalation process.

Follow [#12 subscriptions](https://github.com/jamesjph91/nourish-planner/issues/12), [#7 privacy](https://github.com/jamesjph91/nourish-planner/issues/7), and [#13 beta](https://github.com/jamesjph91/nourish-planner/issues/13).

## 4. Launch staging

| Stage | App | Brand/merch | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| Foundation | v61 PWA and tests, auth/Firestore hardening | Candidate screening, unbranded sample research | Continue |
| Controlled beta | Real-device journeys, privacy and account evidence | Sample fit/quality, validated economics | Only after beta data/security gate |
| Paid app pilot | Secure, tested subscriptions and customer support | Cleared name/identity | Owner approval |
| Coordinated launch | Stable app and measured conversion/retention | Small, tested merchandise run | Owner go/no-go |
| Expansion | Retention and cost controls | Broader range only after repeatable demand | Evidence-led |

No sales forecasts, profitability guarantees or launch dates should be stated without observable beta, cost and pricing evidence. Do not buy inventory merely to appear more commercially complete.
